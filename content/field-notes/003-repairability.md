## The problem

Most of what a repair costs is set on the day of installation. A valve buried behind tile, a heater with no union, a manifold with no labels. None of these fail on day one. All of them turn a one hour repair into a day.

## Seven checks

Each check is yes or no. The count is the repairability score for the installation.

1. **Isolation.** Every fixture and appliance has its own working shutoff.
2. **Disconnect.** Equipment can be removed without cutting pipe.
3. **Access.** Every valve, trap and cleanout can be reached without demolition.
4. **Clearance.** There is room to swing a wrench and pull the part.
5. **Labels.** Valves and circuits say what they serve.
6. **Standard parts.** Wear parts are stocked locally, not special order.
7. **Record.** Photos of the open wall and a simple as-built are on file.

## Example score

| Installation | Score | Time to isolate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Water heater, as found | 3 of 7 | 14 min |
| Water heater, after rework | 7 of 7 | under 1 min |

## Why it matters past this building

Time to isolate is a small number that says a great deal. In a home it is the difference between a wet floor and a flooded one. In a remote or sealed environment, where the people living there make the repair, it is the difference between a nuisance and an emergency. The checks are the same in both.
